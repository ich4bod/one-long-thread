Is this really knitting?

Knitted cloth is made of loops pulled through earlier loops. In weft knitting, a whole piece can come from one yarn. Unsecured loops can be pulled out from the working end: knitters call it ripping back, or frogging. Here the needle and finishing edge are left out so you can get straight to the pulling. The drawing is a loop diagram, not a simulation of yarn tension. Its 192 equal sections are counting units, not measured lengths. Our yarn is dyed section by section; this is not a lesson in changing yarn colors.
